Soul Dust Revival

A palette drawn from the trek-at-sunset key art — ember sky, dust-lit ridgeline, ink-dark figures walking toward the light.

Core — ink & paper
Ink#1a120c
Umber#3a281c
Dusk#6b5344
Paper#f7ecd8

Ink is the dominant dark — near-black but warm, lifted straight from the silhouetted figures and foreground shadow. Paper is a warm off-white (not pure white) for backgrounds and reversed type, echoing the dusty haze in the air rather than a clinical white.

Signal — the sunset itself
Ember 700#b8501f
Ember 500#e2762c
Ember 300#f2a34f
Gold 500#f6c453
Sun 100#fff4d6

This is the brand's one saturated move — the burnt-orange-to-gold gradient of the sky around the sun, ending in the near-white glow at its core. Use Ember 500 as the primary call-to-action color; Sun 100 only as a glow/highlight, never as a large flat fill (it reads as blank at scale).

Dust — earth neutrals
Dust 500#a67c52
Dust 300#c9a66b
Dust 100#e8d3ae
Haze 300#d9c3a3

Pulled from the sunlit dirt and rock in the foreground and the atmospheric haze softening the far ridgeline. This is the workhorse neutral range — cards, dividers, secondary surfaces — so the whole page still feels sun-warmed even where the ember tones aren't present.

How it composes: ink backgrounds with paper type for the moody, cinematic feel of the image at dusk; ember/gold reserved for the sun itself — logo mark, key CTA, a single accent line; dust neutrals for everything structural in between so nothing competes with that one bright point of light in the source photo.
